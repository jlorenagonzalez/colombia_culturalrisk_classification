About the Project

Project Objective

This project aims to automatically classify Colombian municipalities according to the risk of losing their cultural traditions using demographic, social, and cultural data.

Colombia is recognized worldwide for its cultural diversity, traditions, festivals, gastronomy, music, crafts, and indigenous heritage. However, globalization, migration, and social changes threaten the preservation of these traditions.

Why is this Important?

Preserving cultural traditions helps protect national identity, strengthen social cohesion, preserve collective memory, and promote local economic development through tourism and cultural activities.

Several Colombian traditions are recognized by UNESCO as part of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Why Are Traditions Being Lost?

🌍 Globalization

Modern lifestyles and foreign cultural influence reduce interest in traditional practices.

🏙️ Migration

Young generations migrate to cities for work opportunities and stop learning ancestral traditions.

📱 Technology

Digital entertainment and social media replace cultural participation and oral transmission.

⚠️ Lack of Education

Traditional knowledge is not always included in educational systems or community programs.

Municipality Risk Classification

Municipalities can be classified using machine learning models and cultural indicators.

🟢 Low Risk

Municipalities with strong cultural participation, active traditions, preservation programs, and intergenerational transmission.

🟡 Medium Risk

Municipalities where traditions are still present but facing moderate decline due to urbanization and social changes.

🔴 High Risk

Municipalities with endangered languages, disappearing crafts, low cultural participation, and reduced transmission of knowledge.

Data Sources

The project uses public and demographic datasets from Colombian institutions to train classification models and analyze municipalities.

Source Description
