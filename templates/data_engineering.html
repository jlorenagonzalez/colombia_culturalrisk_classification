Dataset Selection & Acquisition

Consolidating a multidimensional telemetry repository for 1,123 Colombian municipalities to mathematically model the degradation parameters of intangible cultural heritage.

Context & Provenance

Real-World Information Fusing

Predicting cultural tradition loss cannot be accomplished via isolated, qualitative sociological surveys. In order to construct a scalable Machine Learning system, we designed a comprehensive matrix containing **{{ total }} rows**, which covers exactly 100% of the political subdivisions (municipalities) of Colombia. The dataset is structurally bound by fusing two real-world governmental institutions data streams:

Socio-Demographic Stream (DANE)

Derived from the National Population Census. It injects structural territorial metrics such as poverty indices (Unsatisfied Basic Needs - NBI), age pyramids, and migratory phenomena. This addresses the socio-economic root causes of community displacement.

Cultural Telemetry Stream (MinCultura)

Extracted from internal public registries of the Ministry of Culture and regional secretariats. It tracks public financial per-capita budget stimulus injections, active community artistic collectives, and historical traditional festival frequency.
