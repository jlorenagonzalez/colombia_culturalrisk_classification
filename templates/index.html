Automatic Classification of Colombian Municipalities According to Cultural Tradition Loss Risk

A Machine Learning project based on the CRISP-ML methodology to analyze demographic and cultural open data and identify Colombian municipalities with potential risk of losing cultural traditions.

Explore CRISP-ML Process
CRISP-ML Phase 1

Business Understanding

This phase defines the real-world problem, the project objective, the expected impact, and the cultural preservation context.

Problem Definition

Colombia has a wide cultural diversity represented through traditions, festivals, music, gastronomy, crafts, languages, and community practices. However, demographic change, migration, globalization, and reduced intergenerational transmission may increase the risk of cultural tradition loss in some municipalities.

General Objective

To classify Colombian municipalities according to possible risks of cultural tradition loss using demographic and cultural open datasets combined with Machine Learning techniques.

Specific Objectives

  • Identify relevant demographic and cultural variables.
  • Collect open data from official Colombian sources.
  • Prepare municipal-level indicators for analysis.
  • Develop a classification model for cultural risk estimation.
  • Present the results through a web-based application.
CRISP-ML Phase 2

Data Understanding

This phase explores the available data sources, their quality, their variables, and their usefulness for cultural risk classification.

DANE Data

Demographic, population, social, and territorial indicators can help explain changes in municipal cultural dynamics.

Open Government Data

Public datasets may provide information about municipalities, education, culture, tourism, and local development.

Cultural Institutions

Cultural heritage records, festivals, traditions, and preservation initiatives can support the analysis of cultural risk.

CRISP-ML Phase 3

Data Preparation

This phase transforms raw data into a structured dataset suitable for Machine Learning model training and evaluation.

Data Cleaning

Missing values, duplicated records, inconsistent formats, and incomplete municipal information must be identified and corrected.

Data Integration

Different datasets must be joined using municipal identifiers, department names, or official territorial codes.

Feature Engineering

New indicators can be created from demographic, educational, social, and cultural variables to represent cultural risk factors.

CRISP-ML Phase 4

Model Engineering

This phase focuses on selecting, training, and improving Machine Learning models to classify municipalities according to cultural tradition loss risk.

Model Selection

Classification algorithms such as Decision Trees, Random Forest, Logistic Regression, or other supervised learning models can be evaluated.

Training Process

The model is trained using demographic and cultural indicators prepared from open datasets at the municipal level.

Risk Categories

The expected output is a risk classification such as Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk.

CRISP-ML Phase 5

Evaluation

This phase validates whether the Machine Learning model provides reliable and useful classifications for cultural risk analysis.

Performance Metrics

Metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score can be used to evaluate the classification model.

Confusion Matrix

A confusion matrix helps compare predicted risk levels against expected or reference classifications.

Interpretation

Results must be interpreted in relation to cultural preservation, data limitations, and municipal context.
