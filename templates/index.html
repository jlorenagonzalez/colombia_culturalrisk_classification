Automatic Classification of Colombian Municipalities According to Cultural Tradition Loss Risk

A Machine Learning project focused on analyzing demographic and cultural open data to identify Colombian municipalities that may face risks related to the loss of cultural traditions and heritage preservation.

Explore Project

Project Overview

Understanding how demographic and social changes can impact cultural heritage preservation across Colombian municipalities.

Cultural Heritage

Colombian municipalities preserve unique traditions, languages, celebrations, and historical identities.

Open Data Analysis

The project integrates demographic and cultural datasets obtained from official Colombian open data platforms.

Machine Learning

Classification models will help identify municipalities with possible cultural preservation risks.

Project Objectives

Main goals and expected outcomes of the research project.

General Objective

To classify Colombian municipalities according to possible risks of cultural tradition loss using demographic and cultural open datasets combined with Machine Learning techniques.

Specific Objectives

  • Collect demographic and cultural datasets.
  • Clean and integrate municipal-level information.
  • Create cultural risk indicators and variables.
  • Train and evaluate Machine Learning models.
  • Visualize municipalities with potential cultural risks.

CRISP-ML Methodology

The project follows the CRISP-ML lifecycle for developing structured and reproducible Machine Learning solutions.

Business Understanding

Define the cultural preservation problem and project goals.

Data Engineering

Data collection, cleaning, transformation, and integration.

Model Engineering

Development and training of classification models.

Evaluation

Validate model performance using metrics and testing strategies.

Deployment & Monitoring

Integration, monitoring, maintenance, and future model updates.
