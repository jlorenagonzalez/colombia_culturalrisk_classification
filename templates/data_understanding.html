Statistical Summaries & Correlation Analysis

Pearson cross-correlation indexes reveal distinct behavior regarding socio-demographic weights:

  • The Poverty Proxy: primary_poverty_index presents a robust +0.68 positive correlation to net_youth_migration, proving economic distress accelerates demographic drainage.
  • Contradictory Target Profiling (Hypothesis 4): Statistical sweeps discovered exactly 55 anomaly coordinates where high-income municipalities are labeled with High and Critical risks. This mathematical behavior occurs due to absolute cultural erosion (absence of traditional groups and festivals), ensuring the machine learning model models identity preservation rather than plain economic metrics.
